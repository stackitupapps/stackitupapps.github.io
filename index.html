💡 How payments work

Stack It Up never holds or moves your money. It makes payment QR codes and links, and opens your own UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm…) to finish the payment. Your UPI app confirms the payment, so always check it there.

Under 18? Use a UPI account set up with a parent or guardian, and ask them before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything you enter is saved only in your own browser on your device. Nothing is sent to any server, so nobody else, not even us, can see it. The one exception is the optional Deep mode in Stax: if you turn it on, the questions you type to Stax go to an online AI. It never sees your entries unless you say so.

What is Stax?

Stax is the assistant in the corner of the screen. Tap Ask Stax to ask about your spending, budget or goals, do quick calculations, learn money basics, or tell it to log an entry or create a goal. It always asks before it changes anything.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just open the site and start. There's nothing to sign up for.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your entries would be deleted, because they live in your browser. Use Settings → Download backup regularly, and Restore backup to get everything back.

Can I use it like an app on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen". It gets its own icon and works offline too.

Does it connect to my bank or UPI?

No, and that's on purpose. You log money yourself, which keeps it private and helps you actually notice your spending.
